Post-Industrial Renaissance

Urban Renewal Design in the Beijing Thermal Power Plant Area Based on the Co-living Concept

zhumuyi0321@connect.hku.hk ↗ Google Scholar ↗ LinkedIn ↗ ORCID ↗

The design site, located in Chaoyang District in Beijing, aims to revitalize the abandoned industrial relics of the Beijing Thermal Power Plant. The project, based on the Co-living concept, provides diverse housing options for digital nomads, students, and entrepreneurs, fostering a unique community atmosphere. By transforming industrial relics into a vibrant residential community, the design embodies the concept of "shared rebirth," symbolizing urban revitalization, historical homage, and optimism for the future.
