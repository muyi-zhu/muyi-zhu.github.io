Planning and Design of Comprehensive Improvement and Upgrading of Urban Space of the North Second Ring Road, Beijing

zhumuyi0321@connect.hku.hk ↗ Google Scholar ↗ LinkedIn ↗ ORCID ↗

The study focuses on the comprehensive improvement and planning of urban space along the North Second Ring Road in Beijing. Through in-depth research and analysis of the North Second Ring Road and its surrounding key areas, the study reveals the problems and challenges in cultural heritage protection, urban traffic organization, public space quality, and urban functional layout in this area.

Based on the requirements of higher-level planning and the development potential of the region, planning strategies are proposed with ecological priority, cultural inheritance, traffic optimization, and functional integration as the main content. The aim is to ensure the green corridor of the capital's ecological security pattern, improve the important nodes of urban service facilities, and position the planning for the inheritance and protection of important windows of the capital's cultural heritage elements. Through strategies such as green ecology, traffic optimization, cultural heritage protection, and urban functional integration, diverse waterfront landscape spaces are created, urban ecological corridors are strengthened, traffic networks are optimized, slow traffic system networks are enhanced, cultural facilities are built to strengthen publicity, culturally oriented design of street spaces is emphasized, land use functional integration is increased, and the utilization and convenience of urban spaces are improved, promoting the enhancement of historical heritage, commercial, and public functions along the route and improving the living environment. Integrating urban landscape, historical culture, and modern life, further enhancing the cultural vitality and charm of the core area.

Finally, through the design of the surrounding area of Deshengmen, detailed design of the Temple of Earth - Yonghe Temple - Imperial College area, typical under-bridge space design of the North Second Ring Road, urban greenway space design, and riverside space design along the North City Moat, the goal is to create an eco-friendly, culturally prosperous, convenient, and functionally integrated modern urban space, creating a better living environment for residents and contributing to the sustainable development of the city.
